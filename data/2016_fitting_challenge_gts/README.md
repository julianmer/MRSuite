# 2016 fitting challenge: ground truth

The ground-truth concentrations of the 28 spectra of the ISMRM MRS study group's 2016
fitting challenge, one sheet per dataset, as the challenge released them.

- Source: M. Marjańska, D. K. Deelchand, R. Kreis, "MRS fitting challenge data setup by
  ISMRM MRS study group", Data Repository for the University of Minnesota (DRUM),
  [doi:10.13020/kw61-3j13](https://doi.org/10.13020/kw61-3j13).
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, as the record states.
- The challenge and its results: Marjańska et al., Magn Reson Med 87(1):11-32 (2022),
  [doi:10.1002/mrm.28942](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.28942).
